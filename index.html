Our story

Seven years ago, it all began with a friendship. Two extroverts with very different interests and hobbies, connected somewhere in the starting of their twenties in a classroom far from home, talking, laughing, and teasing each other. Over time, we shared our little victories, defeats, countless moments and happiness, and stayed strong for each other. Somewhere along the way, that friendship quietly became something deeper. We became best friends, each other’s safe place, biggest supporters, and the person we looked for in every happy moment and every difficult one. In our time together we found, whatever is meant for you will find you, and it won't require you to be anything more than what you already are. Years later, here we are — still each other's favourite person but now ready to begin our greatest adventure.

About the city you're visiting

Kozhikode: Heart of the Malabar Coast

Tucked away along Kerala’s sunny green coast, Kozhikode (also known as Calicut) is famous for its friendly locals and welcoming vibe.It has a fascinating history, too. Back in 1498, Portuguese explorer Vasco da Gama landed right here at Kappad Beach, putting the city at the center of the global spice trade.
Today, it is widely loved as Kerala’s food capital. If you visit, you can’t miss the rich Malabar biryani, the sweet, chewy Kozhikodan halwa, or the lively bakeries lining the famous Sweet Meat Street. Between its calm beaches, vibrant arts and literature, and endless chats over hot chai at local tea stalls, Kozhikode offers a wonderful mix of old-world charm and unforgettable food.
